Analyze the entire codebase of this existing Home Assistant HACS integration before making any changes.

Goal:
Add a battery optimization engine based on linear programming, preferably using CVXPY with HiGHS as solver if compatible with the existing architecture.

Initially, the optimization shall consider ONLY electricity prices. Ignore load forecasts, solar forecasts, weather forecasts, battery degradation costs, and any other factors.

Battery Modes

The optimizer must support the following battery modes:

- standby
    * Charge from excess solar production only.
    * Do not charge from the grid.
    * Do not discharge the battery to household loads or to the grid.

- maxuse
    * Charge from excess solar production only.
    * Discharge the battery to cover household consumption.
    * Do not charge from the grid.
    * Do not export battery energy to the grid.

- charge
    * Charge the battery from solar production and/or the grid.
    * Do not discharge the battery to household loads or to the grid.

- discharge
    * Discharge the battery to cover household consumption.
    * Do not charge the battery from the grid.
    * Do not charge the battery from solar production.
    * Solar production should be used for household consumption.
    * Any solar surplus should be exported to the grid.

- sell
    * Discharge the battery for household consumption and grid export.
    * Any battery surplus should be exported to the grid.
    * Any solar surplus should be exported to the grid.

These modes result in the following allowed energy flows:

Mode        PV→Batt  Grid→Batt  Batt→House  Batt→Grid
-----------------------------------------------------
standby       Yes       No          No          No
maxuse        Yes       No          Yes         No
charge        Yes       Yes         No          No
discharge     No        No          Yes         No
sell          No        No          Yes         Yes

Target Battery Percentage

The following modes shall support a target battery state of charge (SOC):

- charge
- discharge
- sell

Examples:
- charge to 80%
- discharge to 30%
- sell to 20%

standby and maxuse do not use a target SOC.
Assume that maxuse is the default when a target soc has been reached.

Optimization Objective

Using electricity prices as the only input, determine the economically optimal sequence of battery modes over the optimization horizon.

The optimizer should determine:
- when charging is beneficial
- when discharging is beneficial
- when selling/exporting is beneficial
- which target SOC should be reached

The objective is to maximize economic benefit while respecting battery constraints.
